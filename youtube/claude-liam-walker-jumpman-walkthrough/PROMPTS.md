# PROMPTS — what was asked, and by whom

## Narration

Liam, Kokoro `am_onyx`, local. No cloud voice service, no paid generation.
Narration text is in `beat_sheet.json` under `narration_text` per beat and is
the only script; nothing is ad-libbed at render time.

## Visuals

No image generation of any kind. Every frame is either:

- **real gameplay**, captured from the running game (see `CAPTURE.md`), or
- a **Brutalist bookend component** rendered by the toolkit's own Remotion
  chassis — the ClaudeComposerAsk card, the verdict, Your Turn, and the outro.

There are no illustrations, no stock footage, no AI-generated images, no
reconstructed interface mock-ups other than the B00 prompt card, which is
labelled as a reconstruction on screen.

## Human and AI split for the film specifically

**Narasimha decided:** that the film exists at all and what it must prove; that
the reconstruction be labelled rather than passed off as a transcript; the
scope call to ship one chapter honestly rather than five thinly.

**Claude did:** the capture driver, the beat sheet and narration draft, the
coverage inventory, the render pipeline work, and this documentation.

**Claude got wrong, and it is in the film's verdict:** rationing the feather's
charges, which made inspection expensive in a chapter about inspecting; three
attempts at the falling debris; and two confidently wrong diagnoses of a capture
bug before measuring it.

The full split for the game is in `SOURCES.md`.
